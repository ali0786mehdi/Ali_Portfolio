The mental model

RAG is search plus generation.

A model may not know your private or current information. RAG retrieves relevant source text first, then gives that evidence to a model before it writes an answer.

01 / INGEST

Collect knowledge

Load PDFs, websites, databases, or notes while preserving source, permissions, and version metadata.

02 / INDEX

Make it searchable

Split documents into chunks and store their text, metadata, and embeddings in an index.

03 / RETRIEVE

Find evidence

Search the index, filter candidates, and rerank the best evidence instead of sending everything to the model.

04 / GENERATE

Answer with proof

Give the question and selected chunks to a model with a strict grounded-answer prompt and citations.
